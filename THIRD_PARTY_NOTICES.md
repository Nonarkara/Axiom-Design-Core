This integration does not vendor Palette's color dataset or book imagery.

`dictionary-of-colour-combinations` (which credits Dain M. Blodorn Kim's earlier
compilation) for the digital values. If a consuming project copies those data,
preserve the dataset's MIT copyright and permission notice from the
[pinned upstream notices](https://github.com/Nonarkara/palette/blob/5dbe4ed5774bc68919bc68a809195e9ce2523059/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Exhibition roles, shares, search and readings are Dr Non Arkaraprasertkul's
interpretation, not historical assertions about Wada. No affiliation with
Seigensha, the Wada estate or the dataset authors is claimed. No book scans,
cover art or publisher copy is reproduced. Other Axiom lineages and licenses
remain as described in `README.md` and `LICENSE`.
